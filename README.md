# NM Audio

NM Audio is an ad-free music player and remixing platform that gives you full control over how you listen to and customize your audio. Upload your favorite tracks, organize them into playlists, and apply custom styles or remixes on the fly.

## Features

- Ad-Free Listening: Enjoy uninterrupted playback with zero advertisements.
- Custom Uploads: Import your own audio files directly into your library.
- Style & Remix Import: Upload tracks with custom styles to play them back as unique remixes, or listen to the original versions anytime.
- Playback Controls: Full support for standard listening features, including custom playlists, track looping, and shuffle mode.
- Familiar Interface: Inspired by modern streaming platforms for an intuitive and seamless user experience.

## Getting Started

1. Upload Your Music: Select the audio files you want to add to your Steal a Song library.
2. Choose Your Style (Optional): Apply a custom style during import if you want to create a remixed version of your track.
3. Build & Play: Organize your tracks into playlists, hit shuffle or loop, and start listening.
